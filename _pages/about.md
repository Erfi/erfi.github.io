I am a PhD student at the University of Freiburg, working in the <a href='https://nr.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/'>Neurorobotics Lab</a>. My focus is on Reinforcement Learning with applications in robotics. My research explores the intersection of representation learning (successor representations), goal-conditioned RL, and learning from demonstrations (LfD).

I obtained a M.Sc. in Computer Science from the <a href='https://www.uni-freiburg.de/'>University of Freiburg</a> and a dual-degree B.A./B.E. from <a href='https://www.colby.edu/'>Colby</a> and <a href='https://engineering.dartmouth.edu/#'>Dartmouth</a> colleges. Prior to my PhD, I worked as a software/ML engineer in a few startups and research labs, including <a href='https://uptimecm.com/'>Uptime Solutions</a>, <a href='https://engineering.dartmouth.edu/community/faculty/minh-phan#'>Dynamics & Controls Lab at Thayer School of Engineering</a>, and <a href='https://www.psiori.com/'>Psiori</a>. 

When I’m not in the simulation, I enjoy exploring the Black Forest on foot or on a bike—it’s the best way to clear my head for the next big research idea.
